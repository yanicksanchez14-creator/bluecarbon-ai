    Method

    How the kelp numbers are made

    Built from published methods, so every step can be checked.

    1. Every clear imageEach cloud-free Sentinel-2 scene of the quarter, at 20 m. Floating canopy reflects near-infrared light; water absorbs it.
    2. Floating Algae IndexBands 4, 8A and 11 (Hu 2009), with the local ocean background removed, so haze and glint don't read as kelp.
    3. Canopy fractionScaled between a detection threshold and a fully covered pixel, within 2 km of shore, leaving out the surf zone where breaking waves look like floating kelp.
    4. Calibrated to KelpwatchThreshold, full-canopy value and surf-zone width fitted to Kelpwatch (UC Santa Barbara), whose Landsat record is calibrated with divers.
    5. Scored where it wasn't fittedTwo kelp beds are left out of the calibration; their agreement with Kelpwatch is the published accuracy.
    6. Pictures and historyEach year's picture is the median of that summer's clear scenes: Sentinel-2 from 2016, Landsat 5, 7 and 8 before. The canopy before 2016 is Kelpwatch's own record (SBC LTER, CC BY 4.0), shown in teal and never mixed with ours.

    Kelpwatch data: Bell, Cavanaugh et al., Santa Barbara Coastal LTER, kelpwatch.org. Kelp carbon credits are not yet recognised by the major registries. This is a monitoring product: canopy extent and trend for restoration projects and their funders.
