Kelp canopy (beta)

California kelp forests, every quarter

Floating kelp canopy mapped from Sentinel-2 every three months since 2016, calibrated against Kelpwatch's diver-checked Landsat record and scored on kelp beds left out of the calibration.

Method

How the kelp numbers are made

Built from published methods, so every step can be checked.

  1. Every clear imageEach cloud-free Sentinel-2 scene of the quarter, at 20 m. Floating canopy reflects near-infrared light; water absorbs it.
  2. Floating Algae IndexBands 4, 8A and 11 (Hu 2009), with the local ocean background removed, so haze and glint don't read as kelp.
  3. Canopy fractionScaled between a detection threshold and a fully covered pixel, only within 2 km of shore.
  4. Calibrated to KelpwatchThreshold and full-canopy value fitted to Kelpwatch (UC Santa Barbara), whose Landsat record is calibrated with divers.
  5. Scored where it wasn't fittedTwo kelp beds are left out of the calibration; their agreement with Kelpwatch is the published accuracy.

Kelp carbon credits are not yet recognised by the major registries. This is a monitoring product: canopy extent and trend for restoration projects and their funders.
